Each `rewards.json` step also has `zero_variance_frac` (the share of that step's prompt groups whose rollouts all got one reward) and `failure_stages` (failed rollouts by stage: `sim`, `timeout`, `program`, `grader`, `task`); the running job's status line shows them for the latest step and the train's result lists them per step (`step_facts`).
A call of several tasks (a `task` list, or `data` naming several) adds `per_task` to each `rewards.json` step: `{task: {"score_mean", "rollouts"}}`, that step's rollouts of each task and their mean reward (a rollout is attributed by its prompt; one matching no single task is left out).
Each finished train (and one kept at a stop) also writes `practice_rows.jsonl`: one line per rollout of the call, `{task, seed, prompt_sha, program, reward, step, start}` (reward = the score GRPO gave it; start = the adapter the call started from), with the prompts in `practice_prompts.json` (`{prompt_sha: [chat messages]}`). A rollout whose program was written for two seeds in one step is left out. These are training data you generated yourself; `skills/trainer/sft_rows.py practice adapters/<name>` turns them into SFT rows (see SFT below).
