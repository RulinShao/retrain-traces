# embodied-olympics golf-putt — instance i00

You control a simulated Unitree G1 humanoid robot (29 joints, 1.32 m tall, 34 kg; the Embodied Olympics simulator on MuJoCo)
via Python. **Task:** Hole the 3.0 m putt: with one stroke of the putter in your right hand, roll the golf ball that lies on the green into the hole.

This is a *fixed instance*: the same venue and start every time. You may reset and retry as many times as you want.
Your deliverable is **one replayable trajectory**, not a general policy.

## The venue
You stand on the green facing +x, side-on to the putt as a golfer does. The ball lies 0.44 m ahead of your pelvis and -0.12 m to your left (negative: to your right); the hole's centre is 3.0 m from the ball in the +y direction (to your left), so the line of play runs across in front of your feet. The green is level and extends 1.5 m behind the ball (to your right), 3.0 m beyond the hole and 2.0 m to each side of the line of play; around it the ground is 0.1016 m lower. The putter is a blade putter: its grip is held in your right fist at right angles to the forearm, the butt 0.05 m above the fist and the shaft leaving the fist on the other side, so with your arm at rest it points away from your right side, about level with your hand. The head (0.110 m from heel to toe, 0.025 m from face to back, 0.025 m high, 0.35 kg) sits at the shaft's end at a lie of 70 degrees, its toe on the side your forearm points to; its face looks at right angles to both the shaft and the forearm: to your left when the forearm points forwards and the shaft down. There is no wind.

Frame: metres; the origin is on the floor under your pelvis at the start, +x is the way you face at the start, +y is
to your left, +z is up; the floor is z = 0. 

## The rules
**Rule book:** The Rules of Golf (R&A / USGA, 2023): Definitions "Hole", "Holed", "Stroke", "Line of Play"; Rules 8.1b, 9.4, 10.1a, 10.1c, 11.1, 13.2, 13.3a; the Equipment Rules (2023), Part 2 (clubs) and Part 4 (the ball); the USGA Stimpmeter instructions for the green's speed. Where this task departs from it:

- Definition "Hole": the opening is a regular 24-sided polygon inscribed in the 0.108 m circle (flats 0.5 mm inside it, never wider than the official hole) with rigid vertical walls (the liner is only drawn), because the simulator has no cylindrical cavity
- Rule 13.2: there is no flagstick (as when the player has it removed)
- Rule 10.1 and the Equipment Rules on the grip: the putter is fixed rigidly to the right wrist of a robot that has no fingers, its grip at right angles to the forearm (as a fist holds a hammer), and is played with one arm; the club itself is not scaled to the 1.32 m robot
- Rule 10.1a (a fair strike; an accidental double hit is one stroke): the referee needs numbers: every contact of the head with the ball must fall within 0.2 s of the first, and the contacts together may last at most 0.03 s (the simulated impact of a clean putt lasts 0.012 s, a real one 0.0005 s: the ball's contact is softer than a real ball's so that the simulator's 1 ms step resolves it)
- Rules 9.4, 11.1 and 3.3 (penalty strokes, replaying, counting strokes): a single attempt: anything that would cost a penalty or need a second stroke ends it as a failure
- Rule 13.3a (waiting for a ball that overhangs the hole): a ball that has been at rest for 1.0 s is final
- the green: perfectly flat and uniform, no grain, no wind; its speed (stimp 10) is the ball's rolling resistance, calibrated so that a ball rolling at 1.83 m/s stops after 3.05 m

The ball (42.67 mm, 45.9 g) lies at rest on a flat green of stimp 10 (a ball that starts rolling at 1.83 m/s stops after 3.05 m). The hole is cut into the green 3.0 m from the ball: 0.108 m across and 0.1016 m deep, with no flagstick in it. A putter (0.86 m long, 0.5 kg, 3.0 degrees of loft) is fixed in your right hand. You succeed when the ball is holed with one stroke: after the stroke it comes to rest (its centre moves less than 0.001 m in 1.0 s) inside the hole with the whole ball below the surface of the green. A stroke is a fair strike of the ball with the putter's head (any side of the head): the first contact of the head with the ball starts it, every further contact must come within 0.2 s of the first (an accidental double hit is still one stroke), and all the contacts together may last at most 0.03 s. You fail if the contacts last longer (the ball was pushed, scraped or scooped), if the head touches the ball again later (a second stroke), if anything else of you ever touches the ball (the putter's shaft or grip, a foot, a leg, a hand), if at the moment of the stroke your feet touch the green on both sides of the line of play (the straight line through the ball's spot and the hole's centre, extended behind the ball) or on that line, if the ball comes to rest anywhere but in the hole (on the lip and on the green beyond the hole included), if the ball leaves the green, if you fall (your pelvis lower than 0.35 m above the green, or any part of you other than your feet touching the ground; the putter may touch the green), or after 30 s.

A referee decides this from the simulator's state after every physics step (1 ms): the events that must happen, in
order, are **stroke → holed**. The attempt ends at the success (that moment is the success), at the first rule violation,
or after 30 s (3000 control steps). `s.status()` shows the referee's record at any time:
`stages`, `failed` (the rule that ended the attempt), `metrics`, `events`.
