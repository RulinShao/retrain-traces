## decomposition — what a practice sub-goal did and did not do (2026-10-29, #10; staged-reward + start-preset + stage-wise curriculum arc)

On the zero-signal tasks (nut, restack) the binary task-completion reward is all-zero across a GRPO group, so there is no gradient. A sub-goal practice reward + a start-state preset can supply one. What the runs showed (DEV / Qwen3.8-27B; all paths under `$R/pskills/rl/capx_rl/runs/`):

**A REACHABLE sub-goal unlocks the tail.** restack trained FROM a lifted start preset (red grasped+lifted; train align->place) under a staged reward on the tail: binary completions 3/64 -> 8/64, nonzero 13 -> 20/64 over 25 GRPO steps (`train_13962063`). The align->place tail is reachable from lifted, so the policy learns it.

**A sub-goal BEYOND the policy does nothing, and aiming at one wastes capacity.** nut trained FROM a grasped start preset (train align->insert) was completely FLAT — binary 0, nonzero 0, mean 0.0 across all 25 steps (`train_13962061`): nut-align is beyond the 27B policy even from the closest (grasped) start. A nut adapter aimed at align also caused FORGETTING (net-negative: dev lift 77->66, stack 54->51, no gain). Lesson: a staged reward needs a REACHABLE sub-goal; aiming beyond the policy wastes capacity and forgets.

**The preset gain does NOT transfer to from-scratch for free.** Continuing from the preset-trained adapter but training FROM SCRATCH (full chain, no preset) gave 0 from-scratch binary on both: restack hill-climbs to the single easiest sub-goal (unstack, depth-1 54/64) and the within-group variance COLLAPSES 0.015 -> 0.006 -> no gradient past the first barrier (`train_13974952`); nut climbs to reach+grasp (staged 0.027 -> 0.301) but never align/insert (`train_13974953`). Standard dev eval confirms: base restack s4v2 0/100 -> from-scratch adapter 0/100; base nut 0/100 -> 1/100 (noise). The tail-knowing init does not help because the tail state is never reached from scratch.

**Keeping variance alive across a depth MIXTURE is the lever.** A mixed start-state pool (per-GRPO-group mixture of lifted/grasped/scratch, full-chain staged reward so scores SPREAD across depths) keeps within-group variance ALIVE where from-scratch collapsed: restack phase A held var ~0.08 (vs 0.006), staged_mean climbed 0.23 -> 0.35, and the grasped start went from 96% -> 54% program-failure (the curriculum taught it, reaching depth 3-4). Scratch-start was still at the unstack barrier (0 from-scratch binary) as of phase A; the shift-toward-scratch schedule (phases B/C) is in progress (`train_13989823`; mixes under `$R/artifacts/start_presets/mixes/`).
