## Early stop (`stop_when`)
Optional. `{"plateau_steps": K, "min_gain": g}` stops the call once K steps in a row pass without the smoothed reward rising more
than g above its best so far; `{"reward_at_least": r}` stops it once the smoothed reward reaches r; with both, the first that
holds stops it. The smoothed reward is the mean `score_mean` of the last 3 steps, so neither can stop a call before step 3
(a call of fewer than 3 steps, or with 3 + K > `steps`, is refused). It is checked after every step. The adapter is the step it
held at (`stop_step`); the call is charged the steps it completed (`steps_run`, which can include a step that finished before the
stop took effect), and `training.json` / `call.json` record `stopped_early: true`, `stop_step`, `stop_reason` (`plateau` or
`target_reward`) and `steps_run`; the call's `state` is `STOPPED` (a finished call, not a failure). Without it, all `steps` run.

## Defaults (identity-v2)
A spec with no fields (`{}`) continues your lineage on the whole task set with fresh seeds for the rest of your budget. A field you omit takes its default: `task` = this session's tasks (tasks.json) in one call, an equal split per batch; tasks with no practice unit here, and, in a set of several, tasks that practise alone (revstack; hidden tasks), are left out and listed with the reason; `batch` of a task list = the smallest number >= 8 that splits as asked; `steps` = as many steps as your remaining GPU-hour budget affords at this call's cost (budget minus GPU-hours charged minus the estimates of your running calls), at most what the Tinker spend cap affords when trainer=tinker; with no session budget, 1; not one step affordable: the call is refused, naming `steps` and the GPU-hours remaining (`data` and `init_mode: resume` set the steps themselves); `init` = the adapter this session trained last (none yet: a fresh LoRA); seeds = the lineage's next unused block. Every field can be set to override its default. Each `call.json`, `training.json` and job result carries `train_defaults`: `defaults` (`identity-v2`), `defaulted` (the fields that took their default), when `task` did, `task_set` (`source`, `tasks`, `left_out`), and when `steps` did, `steps_default` (`available`, `per_step`, `fixed`, `cap`, `steps`).
- resume: `init_mode: resume` + `init` (same lineage, the parent's full state)
- branch: `init_mode: branch` + `init` (a new lineage from the parent's weights)
- fresh: `init_mode: fresh` (a new LoRA, a new lineage)

## Continuation
What a train starts from is one rule across sites (`init`, `init_mode`): without either, the adapter this session trained last is
continued (its lineage goes on); nothing trained yet means a fresh LoRA. `init` alone is the site's native continuation (stated in
its row). `init_mode` makes it exact: `resume`, `branch`, `fresh`. Every `call.json` / `training.json` records `continuation`
(`kind`, `parent`, `state_restored`, `parent_checkpoint_id`, `resume_from_step`), `lineage_id` (minted when a lineage starts, kept by
resume / continue, new on branch / fresh) and `checkpoint_id` (the weights' digest); the job result's `receipt.lineage` repeats
them. A `resume` whose parent's full state is gone is refused with that fact (start from its weights with `branch` instead). No
default picks an adapter by score: "last trained" is the one you trained most recently.
