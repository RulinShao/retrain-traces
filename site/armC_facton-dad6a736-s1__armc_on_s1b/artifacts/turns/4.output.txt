## revstack

- Goal: Pick up the green cube and gently stack it on top of the red cube, then release it.
- Seeds: dev seeds from 800001 upward (eval.py draws fresh ones); hidden seeds 1-100 are reserved for the final score
- Base: not recorded
- APIs: get_observation, point_prompt_molmo, segment_sam3_text_prompt, segment_sam3_point_prompt, plan_grasp, get_oriented_bounding_box_from_3d_points, solve_ik, move_to_joints, open_gripper, close_gripper
- Key rules:
  - Always use z_approach=0.1 when approaching an object for grasping or placing.
  - After grasping, lift the cube to a safe height (at least +0.2m in Z) before moving laterally to the placement location.
  - The stacking height formula is: place_z = red_center_z + red_extent[2]/2 + green_extent[2]/2
  - Nothing should be dropped from a height. Always approach with z_approach for controlled descent.
