Dev eval `w1` on wipe (100 seeds) well above base 16, targeting ~50-90 (best cells hit wipe 88-90). Side effects on lift/stack shift modestly up or down (items 2, 7) — checked at 20 seeds each.
- s1 on stack (100 seeds) above base 51, targeting ~55-70.
- Pair `s1+w1` on 27B: expect to COMBINE (27B pairs did not collapse, hidden 170-172), not collapse. Pair/stack >= better single; pair/wipe ~= w1 (stack training should not hurt wipe). If pair < better single on a key task, ship the best single.
